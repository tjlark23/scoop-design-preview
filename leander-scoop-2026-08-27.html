  Local Lowdown 
 

Council votes Thursday on the Ronald Reagan overpass design

The city council takes up the final design for the Ronald Reagan Boulevard overpass at its Thursday meeting, the last approval before the project goes out to bid. The current plan adds two through lanes in each direction and a shared-use path on the east side.

Public comment opens at 6:00 PM at City Hall. Staff expect construction to begin in spring and to run about eighteen months.

  Business Brief 
 

Bar W Marketplace signs its first sit-down restaurant

The Bar W Marketplace development at 183A and Bagdad has signed a lease with an Austin-based Tex-Mex group for a 5,400-square-foot restaurant with a patio, the center's first full-service tenant.

The developer says three more leases are close, and expects the first tenants to open before the holidays.

  Restaurant Radar 
 

The Brass Tap's new wood-fired program is quietly the best pizza in town

The kitchen at The Brass Tap on Crystal Falls Parkway added a wood-fired oven in June, and the results are better than they have any right to be. Get the hot honey and soppressata, sit on the patio, and thank us later.

Open 11 AM to 10 PM, later on weekends. The patio is dog friendly and there is usually live music on Fridays.
