🚨 Three hurt in Cedar Park house fire overnight

The Gist: A structure fire broke out in the 3000 block of Paseo in Cedar Park early this morning, injuring three people. Crews responded quickly, but the incident serves as a reminder of fire safety in our neighborhoods.

Why It Matters: House fires can happen to any of us, and knowing your escape routes and having working smoke detectors could make the difference.

🎓 Leander ISD asks voters to raise school tax rate

The Gist: Leander ISD will put a 3-cent Maintenance and Operations tax rate increase on the November ballot. Voters will decide whether to approve the measure to support district operations.

Why It Matters: If you have kids in Leander schools or care about district funding, this November vote directly affects your property taxes and school resources.

🎓 Limegreen Southeast Asian spot opens second location in Leander

The Gist: Limegreen, a locally owned fast-casual restaurant that started near UT's West Campus, opened its second location at 145 South Brook Drive in Leander on August 21. The expansion marks the restaurant's growth after nearly a year at its original spot.

Why It Matters: Local businesses expanding here means more dining options and a sign that Leander's food scene is thriving.
