  Restaurant Radar 
 

🍽️ 🥒 Where Pickleball Players Eat Real Food

Pickledilly's sits inside Apex Pickleball Club in Cedar Park, but this is no clubhouse cafeteria. Executive Chef Ron Matty runs a scratch kitchen where stocks simmer from bones, sauces build fresh each morning, and bread comes from the oven, not a supplier. The menu looks straightforward. The technique behind it is not. Smart cocktails, craft beer, and a wine list that actually thinks about what's on your plate round out the experience. Come in your court shoes or dressed for dinner. Either way, you're getting food made by people who genuinely care.
