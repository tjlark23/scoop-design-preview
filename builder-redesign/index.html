1One top barWorkspace/brand/publication collapse into a single compact picker; the admin banner and newsletter toolbar rows are gone from social mode. The mode switch is one clean segmented control.
2Feed shows content, not controlsSearch + one Filters button (with an active-count badge). Tabs are one row with counts, renamed in your language: News, Events, Places, Homes. Manage sources / Refresh / AI search all live inside Filters or More.
3Hooks live next to the slideThe hook options and caption sit beside the preview instead of below the fold, so choosing the hook is part of looking at the slide, not homework afterwards.
4Slides read as a setBigger thumbs, and the news carousel is visibly varied: photo hook, photo story slide, text-design slide, your closing card. No tripled image.
5Settings are rows, not a junk drawerDesign, closing slide, and saved posts are three labeled rows, always in the same place. Nothing called "More."

Filters are chips, not five dropdownsTap Filters → chip groups in plain language (When / City / Only show) → one "Show 14 events" button. Shuffle, pagination, view toggles: gone. Manage sources and AI search live in the sheet's footer.
Quality states stop shouting"EXTRACTION INCOMPLETE, UNQUALIFIED, STALE" becomes one calm collapsed group: "12 events still being verified." Open it if you want; it never buries the good stuff. Imageless civic items get a designed date card, not a broken-image apology.
One action per cardThe whole card drags; the + adds. "Read" moves to a hover affordance. The green ✓ replaces the "YOU'VE DRAGGED THIS IN" stamp.

What this costsThis is a rebuild of the page's two columns (layout + feed presentation + composer arrangement), not of the engine underneath — templates, generation, picker, downloads all stay as-is. Estimate: 3–4 focused days, shipped behind the same verification screenshots as the last batch.
